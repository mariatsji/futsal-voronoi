Positional Dominance

Every coloured area is the part of the futsal court that is closer to one player than to any other player. That player can reach any spot in his area before anyone else, so he “owns” it. This is called a Voronoi diagram.

  • Blue shades are blue-team players' areas, red/orange shades are red-team players'. Compare the total blue and red space to see which team dominates.
  • Drag a player to see the areas change instantly.
  • Drag a player off the court (into the green margin) and he gets no area, like a substitute on the sideline. His space is taken over by the others.
  • Long-press a player (0.5 s) and drag out an arrow to give him a speed and direction. His area is then drawn around where he is heading, so the space behind a fast player shrinks.

The diagram ignores the ball and only roughly models movement. It is a simple estimate of who controls which space.
